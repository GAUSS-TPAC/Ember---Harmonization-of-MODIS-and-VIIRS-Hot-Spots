How it works

  1. Common grid. Every FIRMS detection (MODIS since 2000, VIIRS since 2012) is counted on 0.1° cells per month, after dropping low-confidence and non-vegetation hotspots.
  2. Transfer factors. On 2012–2025, when both sensors observed, each cell gets a factor k = VIIRS / MODIS (falling back to 1° blocks, then global, where data is sparse).
  3. One record. VIIRS counts are used as is; earlier MODIS months are multiplied by k. The spread of yearly ratios gives the uncertainty band.
  4. Validation. Factors fitted on 2012–2018 and tested on 2019–2025 remove the sensor bias while keeping the seasonal signal.
